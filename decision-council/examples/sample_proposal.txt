I want to build a multi-agent system that automates our EMEA regulatory compliance
review process. The system will use an LLM to parse regulatory documents, identify
requirements, map them to our internal policies, flag gaps, and generate a structured
compliance report for human sign-off.

Currently this process is done manually by a team of 4 analysts, takes 3 weeks per
regulatory change, and costs approximately $2.5M annually in headcount.

The proposed system would reduce this to 4 hours of AI-assisted review + 2 hours of
human validation. Tech stack: Python, Anthropic Claude API, internal document store.
Timeline: 3 months to MVP, 6 months to production.
